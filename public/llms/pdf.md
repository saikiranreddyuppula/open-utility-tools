# PDF

> Merge, split, rotate & build PDFs

5 tools. Each runs entirely in the browser.

Hub: https://www.openutilitytools.com/categories/pdf/

## Tools

- [Image to PDF](https://www.openutilitytools.com/tools/image-to-pdf/): Combine JPG, PNG & other images into a single PDF — one image per page, entirely in your browser.
- [Merge PDF](https://www.openutilitytools.com/tools/merge-pdf/): Combine multiple PDFs into one, reorder by drag — entirely in your browser.
- [PDF Info & Metadata](https://www.openutilitytools.com/tools/pdf-metadata/): Inspect a PDF’s page count, version, and document metadata.
- [Rotate PDF](https://www.openutilitytools.com/tools/rotate-pdf/): Rotate all or selected pages of a PDF by 90, 180 or 270 degrees.
- [Split / Extract PDF Pages](https://www.openutilitytools.com/tools/split-pdf/): Extract or delete a selection of pages from a PDF (e.g. 1,3,5-8).
